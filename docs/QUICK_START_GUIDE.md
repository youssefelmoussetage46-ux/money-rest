# Money Reset Quick Start Guide

Welcome to Money Reset! This guide will help you get started with your personal finance dashboard quickly and easily.

## What is Money Reset?

Money Reset is a beautiful, intuitive personal finance dashboard that helps you understand your money, control your spending, plan budgets, build savings, and track debt—all in one organized place.

## Getting Started

1. **Visit the application** in your web browser
2. **Choose your starting option**:
   - **Start Fresh**: Begin with a clean slate, perfect for new users
   - **Explore Demo**: See how Money Reset works with sample data

## Using the Dashboard

The Dashboard is your financial command center, showing you:
- **Total Income**: All your earnings
- **Total Expenses**: Your spending
- **Current Balance**: Income minus expenses
- **Total Savings**: Progress toward your goals
- **Savings Rate**: Percentage of income you're saving
- **Monthly Overview**: Income vs expenses for the current month
- **Recent Transactions**: Your latest financial activity

## Adding Income

1. Click the **"Income Tracker"** link in the sidebar
2. Click the **"Add Income"** button (+ icon)
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Amount**: How much you earned
   - **Source**: Where the money came from (salary, freelance, etc.)
   - **Description**: Additional details (optional)
   - **Date**: When you received the income
4. Click **"Add Income"** to save

## Tracking Expenses

1. Click the **"Expense Tracker"** link in the sidebar
2. Click the **"Add Expense"** button (+ icon)
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Amount**: How much you spent
   - **Category**: Select from Food, Transport, Bills, Shopping, Entertainment, Health, or Other
   - **Description**: What you purchased (optional)
   - **Date**: When you made the purchase
4. Click **"Add Expense"** to save

## Creating Budgets

1. Click the **"Budget Planner"** link in the sidebar
2. Click the **"Add Category"** button (+ icon)
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Category Name**: Name of your budget category (e.g., "Groceries", "Rent")
   - **Monthly Budget ($)**: How much you plan to spend each month
4. Click **"Add Category"** to save
5. Your budget will automatically track expenses against your limit

## Setting Savings Goals

1. Click the **"Savings Goals"** link in the sidebar
2. Click the **"Add Goal"** button (+ icon)
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Goal Name**: What you're saving for (e.g., "Emergency Fund", "Vacation")
   - **Target Amount ($)**: How much you want to save
   - **Current Amount ($)**: How much you've saved so far (can be 0)
   - **Target Date**: When you hope to reach your goal
4. Click **"Add Goal"** to save
5. Watch your progress bar update as you save more

## Tracking Debts

1. Click the **"Debt Tracker"** link in the sidebar
2. Click the **"Add Debt"** button (+ icon)
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Debt Name**: Name of the debt (e.g., "Credit Card", "Student Loan")
   - **Total Amount ($)**: Original amount owed
   - **Amount Paid ($)**: How much you've paid so far
4. Click **"Add Debt"** to save
5. Track your payoff progress with the visual progress bar

## Using Monthly Overview

1. Click the **"Monthly Overview"** link in the sidebar
2. View your financial trends:
   - **6-Month Financial Trends**: See income vs expenses over time
   - **Monthly Savings Trend**: Watch your savings grow month over month
   - **Income vs Expenses**: Compare this month's income to spending
   - **Expenses by Category**: See where your money goes each month

## Changing Settings

1. Click the **"Settings"** link in the sidebar
2. **Appearance**:
   - **Theme**: Choose between Light and Dark mode
   - **Default Currency**: Select your preferred currency (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, AUD)
3. **Data Management**:
   - **Load Demo Data**: See how the app looks with sample information
   - **Reset All Data**: Clear all your financial data and start fresh (cannot be undone)
4. **Onboarding**:
   - **Restart Onboarding**: See the welcome screen again

## Demo Mode

Want to see Money Reset in action before adding your own data?

1. From the Dashboard, click **"Settings"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Load Demo Data"** in the Data Management section
3. Confirm when prompted
4. Explore the dashboard with sample transactions, budgets, goals, and debts
5. To return to your own data, use the reset function or refresh if you haven't saved anything yet

## Resetting Data

If you want to start completely fresh:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Data Management**
2. Click **"Reset All Data"**
3. Confirm the action when prompted
4. All your financial data will be permanently deleted
5. You'll start with a clean slate, just like when you first used the app

## Need Help?

Remember, all your data is stored locally in your browser's storage. This means:
- Your financial information stays private and secure
- Nothing is sent to external servers
- Clearing your browser data will reset your Money Reset data
- Regularly exporting/backing up is recommended for long-term use
