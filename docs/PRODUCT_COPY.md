## FAQ

### Is my financial data safe and private?
Yes! All your financial data is stored locally in your browser's storage (localStorage). Nothing is ever sent to external servers or databases. Your information stays private and secure on your device.

### Do I need to create an account?
No account required. Money Reset works immediately in your browser with no signup, login, or personal information needed.

### Can I use Money Reset on my phone or tablet?
Yes! Money Reset features a fully responsive design that works beautifully on desktop computers, tablets, and mobile phones.

### What currencies does Money Reset support?
Money Reset supports USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, and AUD. You can set your default currency in the Settings menu.

### Is there a demo mode I can try first?
Absolutely! Click "Explore Demo" when you first visit the app to see Money Reset in action with sample data. This lets you explore all features before adding your own financial information.

### What happens if I clear my browser data?
Since all data is stored locally in your browser, clearing your browser data will reset your Money Reset data. For long-term use, we recommend periodically exporting your data (manual process of recording key information) if you need a backup.

### Can I export my data?
Currently, data export is a manual process where you would record your key information from the dashboard. We're considering built-in export features for future updates based on user feedback.

### How often is Money Reset updated?
We regularly update Money Reset with bug fixes, performance improvements, and new features based on user feedback. Updates are applied automatically when you revisit the application.

### Can I use Money Reset for business finances?
Money Reset is designed primarily for personal finance management. While you could use it for simple business tracking, it's optimized for individual or household personal finances.
